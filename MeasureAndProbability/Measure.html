These notes are the consequences of reviewing Measure Theory from the point of view of a noob in Quantitative Finance. Our principal objective is to describe these notions as a natural mathematical construction born from the many impossibilities in Real Analysis, as language to express events, their probabilities and relationships. We detail whenever the definitions and examples admit a more rigorous explanation or definition than the one we give to the reader. We hope this strategy let appreaciate the reach and pertinence of these concepts avoiding as possible their abstract aspects and mathematical maturity required
